About the CEDS

What is the Bronx CEDS?

A Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy is a locally built, federally recognized plan for a region's economy. The U.S. Economic Development Administration (EDA) supported this one. It sets shared goals, names priority projects, and commits to tracking progress over five years. An approved CEDS also helps the Bronx compete for federal funding.

📊 A SWOT analysis

An honest look at the Bronx economy's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.

🧭 Strategies & priority projects

Two strategic pillars, six goals and an action plan to build inclusive resilience and growth.

📈 A way to measure progress

A framework to track outputs and outcomes. This website and dashboard put it in public view.

How we got here

The CEDS builds on five years of community-led planning.

  1. 2019 · Community-led planning for a better Bronx future begins.
  2. 2024 · Bronx-wide coalition meetings, worker cooperative town halls and the Sustainable Economy Partnership.
  3. 2025 · A year of public engagement: surveys, sector focus groups, public assemblies and interviews.
  4. March 2026 · Public Draft released for a 30-day public comment period.
  5. June 2026 · Inaugural Bronx Economic Development Summit.
  6. 2027 · Implementation begins. This platform launches. You are here

How status works

Plain statuses. Staff enter them from each lead's update and a publisher approves them. Definitions are agreed in Discovery:

  • Not started: in the plan, no work yet
  • Underway: work and a lead are in place
  • Complete: completion evidence accepted
  • Paused: on hold, often for a partner or funding
  • Unknown: no accepted update yet; never assumed complete
Design note: We avoid “% complete” scores for actions that are not linear (councils, advocacy). Progress bars show only milestones met / milestones defined, so the number always ties back to a list the public can read.

Do Business in the Bronx

Opportunities tied to the CEDS

The plan points to specific openings for businesses, entrepreneurs and investors. Each card leads to a contact or program.

🏭

Industrial Business Zones

High-value sector recruitment for Bronx IBZs, with logistics and distribution at the center (Actions 6.1, 6.2).

🤝

Sell to anchor institutions

Supply-chain pathways that connect M/WBEs, cooperatives and social enterprises to hospitals and universities (4.3).

🔑

Succession & employee ownership

Help for owners nearing retirement who want to sell to their employees (4.4).

⚡

Decarbonization contracting

Pooled demand for electrification and retrofit work, with local contracting (3.1.1, 4.5).

💵

BXEDC business loans

Small business lending and technical assistance from the Bronx Economic Development Corporation.

🚀

Entrepreneur cohorts

Cohort programs for new businesses in high-road and high-value sectors (6.4).
